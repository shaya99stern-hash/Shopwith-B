# Actual Clothing Pairings - H&M / Zara / Mango Starting Set

These are the first real outfit pairings to replace the vague placeholders. They are built from the Instagram audit direction: outfit prep, Shavuos/table edits, browns/blues, kids shoes, rompers, family looks, and budget-aware shopping.

## 1. Shavuos Mom Ivory

- Zara embroidered midi dress, layered for neckline/sleeve coverage if needed
- H&M powder-blue embroidered-detail knit cardigan
- Zara ivory closed ballet flats or Mary Jane flats
- Pearl studs

Why it fits: soft holiday palette, modest, refined, not trend-random.

## 2. Family Look Blue

- Mom: Mango navy A-line or fine-knit midi skirt + soft blue blouse
- Girl: Zara Kids blue floral dress + H&M cardigan
- Boy: H&M navy fine-knit polo + H&M twill chinos
- Shoes: Mary Janes for girl, loafers/sneakers for boy

Why it fits: coordinated family palette without looking costume-matched.

## 3. Outfit Prep Girl

- Zara Kids floral print dress
