# Come Shop With B Style Profile

Based on the public Instagram profile at `@comeshopwithb`.

## What The Feed Is Really Doing

This is not generic modest fashion. It is personal-shopper curation for religious family life. The value is not only "cute outfit." It is reducing decision fatigue for moms who are shopping across many people and occasions.

The feed themes include:

- Swim
- Shavuos inspo
- Family look
- Rompers
- SS 26
- Outfit inspo
- Kids sets
- Kids shoes
- Sukkos table
- Tablescape

The mind behind it is practical and emotional at the same time:

- "I found the combination so you do not have to."
- "Here is how to make the family look coordinated without being matchy in a bad way."
- "Here is how not to waste money buying clothes."
- "Here is a seasonal palette that feels exciting again."
- "Here is an outfit-prep system that lowers morning arguments."
- "Here are holiday/table/shopping ideas that feel pretty but still reachable."

## Style Rules

- Modest first, not as an afterthought.
- Family coordination matters.
- Kids categories are not side content; they are core content.
- Shoes matter a lot, especially kids' shoes.
- Rompers and baby outfits are important.
- Holiday shopping matters: Shavuos, Sukkos, Shabbos, family photos.
- The palette should feel seasonal and curated.
- Browns and blues are a known strong direction.
- Spring/summer should be softer: powder blue, ivory, blush, sage, pale yellow, gentle florals.
- Avoid generic influencer trends that do not solve a family-shopping problem.

## Hard Avoids

- Women's shorts
- Women's pants as a core look
- Open-toe shoes
- Belts
- Visible elastic-waist styling
- Low necklines
- Sleeveless women's tops unless layered and still modest
- Random trend pieces with no family use case

## App Implication

The app should not just generate "outfits." It should generate family-shopping edits:

- one mom look
- one sibling/family look
- one kids shoe edit
- one baby edit
- one boys sports durability edit
- one husband edit
- one holiday or table crossover

Each edit should have:

- visual board
- product slots
- store URL
- ShopMy affiliate URL
- tracking label
- status: idea, sourced, affiliate linked, posted, measured
